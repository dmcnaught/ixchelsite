Stay Policies

💳 Booking & Payment

  • May 01 - Dec 20: 50% deposit due at booking. Balance due 15 days prior.
  • Dec 21 - Apr 30: 50% deposit due at booking. Balance due 30 days prior.
  • Note: Credit card charges will appear as "OPER HOTELERA".

📅 Cancellation Policy

  • 31+ days prior: Free cancellation / Full refund.
  • 30 days or less: 100% penalization (Non-refundable).
  • No Shows: Non-refundable.

🛎️ General Information

  • Check-in: 3:00 PM | Check-out: 12:00 PM
  • Requirements: Government ID and credit card/cash deposit for incidentals required at check-in.
  • Rooms: Assigned at check-in. Specific rooms/floors not guaranteed.

ℹ️ Hotel Rules

  • Pets: Restricted (Only service dogs with documentation).
  • Visitors: Restricted to lobby until 10:00 PM.
  • Quiet Hours: Enforced after 10:00 PM. No parties.
